Repair or replace?

It depends on the size of the damage, where it is, and whether it's spreading.

Usually repairable

A small chip or bullseye

Caught early and away from the edge of the glass, a chip can often be filled and sealed so it stops spreading.

We'll look at it

A short crack

Whether it can be repaired depends on its length and how close it is to the edge or to the driver's view.

Usually replaced

Long cracks or damage in your view

Damage that spreads across the glass or sits in front of the driver is safer to replace.

This is general guidance. An EGP technician checks the actual damage and tells you which option fits. Don't risk driving with impaired vision.

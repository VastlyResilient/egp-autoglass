How it works

Quote. Schedule. Fixed.

As a trusted auto glass shop based in Meriden, we take a meticulous approach to every replacement. We work quickly, but we're thorough. Your safety is our top priority, and we won't send you back on the road until we're confident your car is in proper shape. With convenient hours and reasonable replacement rates, call us today for an estimate.

  1. Quote

    Get an instant price online, or call. We match any written quote from another shop.

  2. Schedule

    Confirm the glass for your vehicle and pick a time. If insurance is paying, we handle the claim with your carrier.

    A certified installer replaces the glass at your location. All work is guaranteed, with a limited lifetime warranty.

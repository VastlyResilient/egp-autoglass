Your right to choose

In most states, it's illegal for an insurance company or glass provider network to steer you toward a specific glass company, no matter how subtle the suggestion.

You always have the right to choose the company that repairs or replaces your vehicle's glass. When you choose EGP Auto Glass, you get a limited lifetime warranty, decades of experience, and a company that values your business.

Insurance made easy

EGP Auto Glass is recognized by major insurance carriers as an approved provider of windshield repair and replacement. We're experienced with insurance claims and keep the process simple for you.

We work with your insurance company to get the repair scheduled and completed quickly, and we make sure all billing and account information is handled properly.
